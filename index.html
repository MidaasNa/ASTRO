How this is calculated. Planet positions come from the astronomy-engine library (accurate to about an arcminute). They are converted to the sidereal zodiac with Lahiri (Chitrapaksha) ayanamsa, as the book uses. Houses are whole signs counted from the lagna, as chapter 7 insists. Rahu and Ketu use the mean node. The book says only that a planet “too close” to the Sun is combust, so the standard classical orbs are used for combustion.

Where the words come from. Sign, planet, house, yoga, dasa, transit and remedy texts are taken from the book’s tables and definitions. The book leaves detailed “planet in house” results to other classics, so each planet reading is assembled from the book’s rules: dignity, functional nature, house category, lordship, aspects and significations. It is not quoted from a result table.

Birth time matters. Chapter 32 notes that a 5-minute error shifts dasa dates by about 25 days when Venus starts the sequence, and it can change the lagna, navamsa and D-10. Remedies are traditional practices, not medical, financial or legal advice. The author suggests learning mantra pronunciation from a priest or competent astrologer.
